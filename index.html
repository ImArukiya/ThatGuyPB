faq

When do you stream?

Almost daily — no fixed schedule, just constant. Check the announcement bar at the top or follow on Twitch to get notified the moment a stream goes live.

What's TGC?

ThatGuyPB's Community — the name for everyone here. The Discord is where TGC actually lives between streams. If you're here, you're here to stay.

What games do you stream?

Variety — whatever's in the backlog or whatever chat votes on. Jokes are on the edgy side and there's zero pretension. The genres section above has the live breakdown pulled from recent streams.

What happened to @HHThatGuyPB?

Lost the old account. No pause, only resume — started fresh as ThatGuyPB_ and brought the community over. The channel is gone but TGC survived.

Can I donate? Does it do anything?

Donos are TTS — your message gets read on stream. You absolutely don't have to, it's appreciated either way. Every bit counts toward the goals shown on the Twitch page.

What are the stream rules?

Jokes are fine, just don't be a genuine dick about it. Have a good time. If you're here you're here to stay — welcome to TGC.

Where do VODs go?

Twitch VODs stay up on the channel and the recent episodes carousel above pulls them automatically. YouTube has the longer cuts too — links are in the nav and footer.

How do I join the community?

Discord link is in the footer and the community section above. Follow on Twitch for live notifications. Show up in chat and say something — nobody bites.
