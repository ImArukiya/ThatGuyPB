POLICY

What you're allowed to do with clips, highlights, and VODs from ThatGuyPB streams. Personal use is almost always fine — commercial use needs a conversation first.

00 — OVERVIEW
The Basics

TGC content — livestreams, clips, highlights, VODs, and any derivative content like compilations — belongs to ThatGuyPB. That said, the community sharing moments from the stream is part of what TGC is. This policy exists to make the line clear: personal sharing is encouraged, commercial exploitation is not.

When in doubt, just ask. Reach out on Discord or Twitter/X. The answer is usually yes with credit.

01 — WHAT YOU CAN AND CAN'T DO
Permissions at a Glance
ALLOWED
Personal & Community Use
Sharing clips directly on Discord, Twitter/X, Reddit, or anywhere social — no permission needed
Uploading short clips (under ~5 minutes) to personal social accounts with credit to ThatGuyPB
Making personal compilations for fun and sharing them non-commercially
Embedding clips or VODs in blog posts, articles, or fan sites without monetisation
Using clips in non-profit tribute or fan edit videos with clear attribution
Reacting to VODs or clips on your own stream — keep the original audio intact, don't crop out branding
NOT ALLOWED
Commercial & Exploitative Use
Uploading full VODs or long-form streams to YouTube or any platform and monetising them
Including clips in commercial products, ads, or sponsored content without written permission
Selling compilations or any product that uses TGC content
Using clips out of context to misrepresent ThatGuyPB's views or character
Re-uploading content and presenting it as your own without attribution
Using ThatGuyPB's likeness, name, or branding in merchandise without permission
02 — SPECIFIC SCENARIOS
Common Questions Answered
✓
Clip compilations on YouTube (no ads) Fine. Put "ThatGuyPB" in the title or description and link back to the Twitch channel. Don't monetise it.
✓
Using a clip in a video essay or analysis Fine under fair use principles — as long as the use is genuinely commentary, criticism, or educational. Don't pad a video with long clip segments just to fill runtime.
✓
Reacting to clips on your stream or YouTube Fine. Keep the original audio, include "ThatGuyPB" in the video description with a link. If you're monetised, that's fine as long as the clip is a small portion of your own content.

Using a clip in a paid advertisement or sponsored post Needs written permission first. Reach out via Twitter/X or Discord before you go live with it.

03 — ATTRIBUTION
How to Credit Properly

When sharing or re-posting TGC content, attribution should look something like this:

Clip from ThatGuyPB — twitch.tv/thatguypb_
YouTube: @HHThatGuyPB · VODs: @ThatGuyPBVods

That's it. You don't need to write an essay — just make it clear where the content came from and give people a way to find the original.

If you're sharing on a platform that doesn't support links well (TikTok, Instagram Stories), including the name "ThatGuyPB" in text or on screen is enough.

04 — MUSIC IN STREAMS
Third-Party Music on Stream

ThatGuyPB streams sometimes include third-party music. If you clip or re-upload content that contains third-party music, you may receive copyright claims from the music rights holders — not from ThatGuyPB. That's between you and the rights holder.

ThatGuyPB is not responsible for any copyright strikes, content ID matches, or takedowns you receive as a result of re-uploading stream content that includes licensed music. If you're worried about this, mute audio during music sections before re-uploading.

05 — ENFORCEMENT
What Happens if This Policy Is Violated

For minor or accidental violations — a clip uploaded without credit, a compilation that got monetised by accident — a friendly DM is usually the first step. Fix it and move on.

For deliberate commercial exploitation, using content to misrepresent or harass, or repeated violations after being asked to stop, a DMCA takedown request or platform report may be filed.

TGC is a community built on good faith. This policy exists because it's needed, not because we're looking for people to go after. Operate in good faith and it won't be a problem.
