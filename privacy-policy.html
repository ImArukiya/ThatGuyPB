PRIVACY
POLICY

What this site collects, what it doesn't, and how any data is used. Plain language — no legalese walls.

00 — OVERVIEW
The Short Version

This is the personal website of ThatGuyPB, a Twitch streamer. It's a static site hosted on Cloudflare Pages. It does not run any databases, does not have user accounts, and does not collect or store personal data on its own servers.

Where third-party services are embedded or loaded — Twitch, Google Fonts, Streamlabs — their own privacy policies apply. Those are listed below.

If you're just browsing this site, reading content, and not interacting with any embedded services, essentially no personally identifying data about you is collected by this site.

01 — WHAT THIS SITE COLLECTS
Data Collection

This site collects the following data directly:

TYPEWHATWHYSTORED?
Cloudflare Analytics Anonymised page view counts, referrers, browser type, country. No IP addresses are stored. To understand roughly how many people visit and from where. No ad targeting, no profiling. By Cloudflare, per their privacy policy. Not accessible as individual records.
localStorage (browser) Music player position and volume settings (stored entirely in your own browser). To remember where you left the music player widget on the page. Only in your browser. Never sent to any server.
Twitch API calls This site calls Twitch's API via a Cloudflare Worker proxy to show live stream status, follower counts, and VOD data. To display real-time channel information. The API calls are server-side — your IP is not forwarded to Twitch. Responses are not cached beyond the request. No user data is involved.
No cookies are set by this site directly. Third-party embeds (Twitch player, Twitch chat) may set their own cookies when they load. You can block these at the browser level.
02 — THIRD-PARTY SERVICES
Third Parties Loaded on This Site

These services are loaded by or embedded in this page. Each has its own privacy policy which governs how they handle your data.

Twitch
The live stream embed and chat embed load when the channel is live. Twitch may set cookies. Their policy: twitch.tv/p/legal/privacy-policy

Patreon
Links to TGC Hideout on Patreon. Patreon handles all subscription data. Their policy: privacy.patreon.com/policies
Cloudflare Pages

03 — CHILDREN
Age and Audience

This site is not directed at children under 13. The stream content is intended for a general adult audience and contains mature humour. If you are under 13, please don't use this site.

If you believe a child under 13 has somehow submitted personal data via this site, contact us and we'll address it immediately — though given the site has no forms or account systems, this is effectively impossible through normal use.

04 — YOUR RIGHTS
GDPR, CCPA, and Your Rights

Since this site does not collect personally identifying information directly, most data rights requests (access, deletion, portability) don't apply to data held by this site — because there isn't any.

For data held by third-party services listed above, contact those services directly. They each have mechanisms to handle rights requests under GDPR (for EU residents), CCPA (for California residents), and other applicable laws.

If you have a question about data and this site specifically, contact via Twitter/X @ThatGuyPB.

05 — LINKS
External Links

This site contains links to Twitch, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Twitter/X, Patreon, and Streamlabs. Clicking those links takes you to third-party sites governed entirely by their own privacy policies. We have no control over what they collect.

We're not responsible for the privacy practices of sites we link to. When you leave this page, you're on their terms.

06 — CHANGES
Updates to This Policy

If this policy changes materially — for example if analytics or embedded services change — this page will be updated and the date below will reflect it. For significant changes, an announcement will go out in the TGC Discord.
